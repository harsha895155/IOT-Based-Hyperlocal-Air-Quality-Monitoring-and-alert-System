AirGuard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026 • Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Welcome to AirGuard ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and delivering clear transparency regarding how your data is handled across our environmental monitoring web platform and Android mobile application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Approximate Location: Used solely to fetch localized micro-climate forecasts via Open-Meteo. We do not track continuous GPS movement or store background location histories.
  • Hardware Telemetry: Real-time MQ135 gas concentrations, Air Quality Index (AQI), and DHT11/22 temperature and humidity values transmitted by your connected ESP32 sensor hardware.
  • Account Data: If registering or signing in via Google OAuth, we store your name and verified email address solely to associate your hardware nodes and alert configurations.

2. No Third-Party Ads or Data Sales

Zero Advertising • No Data Monetization

AirGuard does not show third-party ads, contains no tracking advertising SDKs, and will never sell, rent, or trade your personal or environmental telemetry records.

3. Data Security & Storage

All communication between hardware nodes, our servers, and client applications is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Authentication tokens are cryptographically secured using JWT.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account, device telemetry records, and personal information at any time. Simply use the in-app profile settings or contact our support team.
